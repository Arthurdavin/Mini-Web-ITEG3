Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book a tour?

You can book a tour directly through our website, send us an email, or give us a call. We'll confirm availability and send you a booking confirmation within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, bank transfers, and cash payments at our office.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour start time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance are non-refundable.
