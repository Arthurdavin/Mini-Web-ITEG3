About Explore Cambodia

Your trusted partner in discovering the wonders of Cambodia since 2011

Our Story

Founded in 2011 by a group of passionate Cambodian travel enthusiasts, Explore Cambodia was born from a simple dream: to share the beauty, culture, and warmth of our homeland with the world.

What started as a [20px]all operation with just three tour guides has grown into one of Cambodia's most trusted travel agencies, serving over 50,000 travelers from around the globe.

Despite our growth, we've never lost sight of what matters most: creating authentic, meaningful experiences that showcase the real Cambodia.

Today, we're proud to employ over 100 local guides, drivers, and support staff, all dedicated to making your journey unforgettable.
